The art of paying attention

A walk without a destination can change the shape of a day.
Nora Ellis
October 10, 2026
8 min read

Opening note

We had walked this street a hundred times. That morning, with no appointment ahead of us, it seemed to contain an entirely different city.

There was a seam of light beneath the bookshop door, a pear tree leaning over a wall, the sound of someone practising a difficult piece of music. Nothing had changed. We had simply begun to notice.

“Attention is a way of making room.”
