# Typography studio: experimental guidance

This is an original, deliberately small candidate method, not an installed skill. Its purpose is to make typography feel intentional in a specific setting. Measurements diagnose problems; they do not certify beauty.

1. Name the intended voice in three concrete words and assign text roles before choosing sizes. In refinement mode, identify what already carries the design's identity and preserve it. Change the few relationships that matter most.
2. Choose the primary face for the actual words and role. Look at its lowercase proportions, terminals, width and apparent darkness. A family label such as serif is not a voice. Use a companion only when its role contrast earns the added texture. One family can provide substantial hierarchy.
3. Make the first reading unmistakable: title, supporting statement, utility, sustained text. Establish one decisive contrast, then keep secondary steps quieter. Compare apparent size and darkness, not merely numerical ratios. Avoid making every role emphatic.
4. Compose the exact title as a shape. Inspect the rag, phrase breaks, interline space and punctuation. Tighten display rhythm only while counters and adjacent lines remain clear. Do not force a headline break simply to make a geometric block.
5. Set body text as a continuous texture. Evaluate size, measure and baseline interval together using actual lines. Start with the face's default tracking for prose. Use space or indentation to separate paragraphs intentionally. A long measure may need more leading; a short interface label does not inherit prose rules.
6. Group related text optically: small internal gaps, clearer separation between sections. Inspect capitals, numerals and mixed fonts for apparent baseline/weight mismatch. Use wider tracking sparingly for short uppercase utility, not as a universal polish filter.
7. Render at the intended size. Compare alternatives with identical copy and geometry. Record a specific gain and a specific cost for each. Keep an unchanged treatment or a tie when revision adds novelty without improvement. For audits, show before/after and explain the preserved identity.
8. Check real font loading, missing glyphs, clipping, unintended final-word orphans and rendering differences. Reading access is a guardrail: reflow, enlarged text and contrast must be evaluated before production, but are not substituted for aesthetic review.

## Evidence and limits

- [Inter specimen](https://rsms.me/inter/), viewed 2026-10-10: a forceful, tightly set display heading and quieter utility/prose demonstrate role contrast within one family. This is a visual observation, not a required recipe.
- [Source Serif specimen](https://adobe-fonts.github.io/source-serif/), viewed 2026-10-10: parallel weight specimens expose markedly different page texture. The project documents optical sizes; allow automatic optical sizing and judge the resulting size rather than treating all cuts as interchangeable.
- [Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/), viewed 2026-10-10: diverse book identities coexist with a restrained wrapper. Inference: distinguish identity-bearing display roles from navigation roles. Do not copy the books' compositions or assets.
- [IBM Plex concept](https://www.ibm.com/plex/concept/): the designer's account connects letterform details to brand character. Inference: choose distinctive details that fit the brief; do not equate distinctive with loud.
- [Butterick, line spacing](https://practicaltypography.com/line-spacing.html) and [line length](https://practicaltypography.com/line-length.html): useful starting ranges for prose, not universal pass/fail criteria. The pilot does not optimize to a magic ratio.
- [Apple, The details of UI typography](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10175/): native font metrics and platform behavior require their own implementation. A CSS baseline multiplier is not SwiftUI's additional line spacing.

No full typography book text was supplied or ingested. Broader book grounding remains a research requirement for a final skill; this method does not claim to reproduce Bringhurst, Lupton or any author's complete system. These short principles are original synthesis and explicitly labeled contextual judgment.
