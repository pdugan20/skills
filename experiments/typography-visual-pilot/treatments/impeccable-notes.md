# Impeccable reviewed-guidance treatment

All six treatments are proposed designs. They have not been rendered or visually verified.

## Guidance applied

Source: [archived typeset reference](../inputs/impeccable-typeset.txt), read as design guidance only.

- **Authority and fit:** retain Inter throughout product-refine, Source Serif 4 reading roles with Inter utilities in editorial-refine, and system sans utilities with system serif title/body in native-refine. Product-new uses only IBM Plex Sans; editorial-new uses Source Serif 4 for reading and Inter for publication information. Native-new uses platform sans and serif roles.
- **Visitor mode:** prioritize scanning and predictable density in the workspace; allow the essay title to carry more voice; preserve a familiar native utility hierarchy in the saved story.
- **Role system:** use the nine required roles as a fixed contract. Brand identifies the publication, eyebrow orients the reader, title anchors the surface, deck introduces content, body carries reading, meta supplies secondary facts, section organizes content, item carries stories/aside/action, and value emphasizes data. Size, weight, leading and space work together rather than relying on size alone.
- **Role-specific leading:** replace the supplied universal 1.45 multiplier with compact display leading, moderate utility leading and more open prose leading. Editorial body uses 1.55 at 20px; native body uses 1.48–1.50 at 17–17.5pt.
- **Reading size:** ordinary web body is at least 16px. Smaller label and metadata roles are deliberate utility exceptions, not prose sizes.
- **Rag and rhythm:** propose a phrase break after “The art of” for editorial/native titles, modest negative display tracking and small positive utility tracking. Paragraph spacing is the sole authored paragraph boundary treatment. Workspace row spacing stays restrained.
- **Few families and continuity:** each family has a distinct functional purpose. Refinements keep original headline/body sizes except the product body floor adjustment, relying mainly on spacing, tracking and leading; new designs allow somewhat stronger scale contrast.

## Limits and evidence

Only the brief, supplied before data, repository AGENTS.md and the reviewed source above informed the treatment. No other treatment files, specialized skills or outside references were loaded. No detector, launcher, setup, mechanical scan, polish workflow or installation was run.

The contract preserves copy, palette, content order and major layout by exposing only role styling, the five permitted spacing values and the permitted title-break choice. The JSON structure and allowed values were checked locally. That source validation is not visual evidence.

The intended prose measure is the guidance’s approximately 45–75 characters on the web, with a naturally shorter native measure. Container width is fixed outside this contract, so actual measure, line endings, headline fit, optical alignment and canvas overflow remain unverified. The two-line title intent especially depends on the remaining phrase fitting the rendered container.

The contract does not expose font features, font loading, fallback metrics, optical sizing, responsive scaling, browser zoom, Dynamic Type, localization or contrast controls. Those behaviors were neither changed nor tested. No live-mode scale parameter was added because it is outside the required JSON schema. No dark-surface compensation was assumed without inspecting the actual rendering.
