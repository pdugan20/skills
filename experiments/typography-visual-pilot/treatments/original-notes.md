# Original guidance arm — proposed treatments

These six proposals use only the experiment brief, shared before data and lightweight original guidance. No other treatment outputs, external skills or candidate code were consulted. Copy, palette, content order and major layout are unchanged by this data. Rendering has not been performed: the gains below are intentions, not observed outcomes.

| Case | Voice and identity | Intended gain | Specific cost / next visual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| product-new | Observant, orderly, independent. Plex handles the working system; Source Serif appears only in the introductory deck. | A moderate-weight, larger issue heading establishes first reading, with story names more prominent than column labels and restrained numerals. | The serif deck adds texture and another family; verify that it feels like the publisher's voice rather than an unrelated editorial flourish. Inspect numeral darkness and table-row alignment. |
| product-refine | Calm, direct, capable. Preserve all-Inter identity, brand and story-name treatments. | Compress the issue heading and numeral line boxes; soften column labels and group the introductory sentence more closely. | Smaller, lighter column labels may become too recessive. Compare the untouched row rhythm against before; do not assume every unchanged role needs revision. |
| editorial-new | Attentive, warm, assured. Source Serif carries the reading voice; Plex is the journal wrapper. | Phrase-based title break and close leading make the heading a deliberate two-part statement; open prose creates a slower reading texture. | The long second phrase may wrap again at the fixed measure. Check that rag and the larger prose fit the canvas without crowding the aside or footer; the forced break is provisional. |
| editorial-refine | Literate, composed, clear. Preserve every family, size and weight from before. | Distinguish display, supporting and sustained-reading rhythms without replacing the existing identity. | Looser body leading adds height. Verify the natural headline wrap and interline clearance, then compare total section balance with before. |
| native-new | Companionable, thoughtful, familiar. Georgia gives the story its character; system sans provides familiar app utility. | A regular serif title and robust prose make a welcoming reading companion, with compact grouping and more legible metadata. | Georgia may feel darker or wider than the system serif. Inspect actual native metrics, title rag, deck-to-body transition and the final paragraph's fit before accepting it. |
| native-refine | Quiet, readable, familiar. Preserve system sans utility, serif title/body and original display weight. | Bring the opening into one group, lift metadata size, and give prose a small increase in breathing room. | The heavier serif headline may need more clearance than the proposed leading permits. Confirm line collisions, total height and enlarged-text reflow on the actual native renderer. |

Default tracking is retained for prose throughout. Positive tracking is limited to brief uppercase roles or small introductory labels; it is not applied to general utility or body text. New designs take stronger positions; refinements preserve identity and concentrate changes on display rhythm, hierarchy and related spacing.

All nine required roles are specified for every case, including roles whose use depends on the shared template. `leading` follows the experiment's multiplier contract; these values are not a claim that CSS line height and SwiftUI additional line spacing are interchangeable. No rendering, font-loading, glyph, clipping, rag, optical-alignment or enlarged-text check is claimed complete. Visual comparison may justify restoring an unchanged value or calling a tie.
