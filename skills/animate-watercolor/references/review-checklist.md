# Review checklist

Use this after making a bounded watercolor change. Record evidence rather than
checking boxes from memory.

1. **Accepted reference:** Identify the preserved artwork, preset and checkpoint.
   If matching another subject, read and render its current implementation.
2. **Actual technique:** Name which layers use authored paths, palette deposits,
   source glazes, coverage masks and residual interpolation.
3. **Critical regions:** Inspect before/during/after the reported discontinuity.
   Large coverage and geometry must exist before the final texture settles.
4. **Timing:** Convert the relevant score intervals into visible milliseconds.
   Review normal speed as well as slow motion; do not infer either from the other.
5. **Themes:** Check local whites, clear glass and edge pixels against both real
   site backgrounds. Confirm bloom color and blur behavior.
6. **Composition:** Verify crop, proportions and placement at desktop and a narrow
   viewport. Test actual display-scale bubble visibility, not only a zoomed canvas.
7. **Lifecycle:** Replay repeatedly, interrupt, exit/re-enter, change subjects while
   preparing, handle failure/retry, suspend hidden-tab time and honor reduced motion.
8. **Ambient:** Bubbles persist during the hold; the droplet stops at its authored
   endpoint and does not loop. Both restart with a new painting.
9. **Shared system:** Recheck the existing subject when changing common code.
   Keep settings independent and avoid a duplicate player or control panel.
10. **Evidence:** Save source/prompt provenance, chosen parameters, relevant frames
    or recordings, known limitations and the checks actually run. Never use a
    passing unit test as evidence that motion looks natural.

The outcome is a working comparison and a defensible checkpoint. If aesthetic
acceptance is still pending, say so without stopping unrelated authorized work.
